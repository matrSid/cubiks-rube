    Tap once to start a 15-second countdown, look over the cube, then hold and release to start solving. Starting after 15 seconds adds +2. Starting after 17 seconds counts as DNF.

    At 15 seconds the solve timer starts on its own, with no penalty. You can still hold and release earlier to start sooner.

    Only used during inspection.

    A countdown that runs before inspection (or before the solve, if inspection is off), so you can set the cube on the mat. Tap during the countdown to skip it.
